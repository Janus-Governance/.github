# Code of Conduct

Janus Governance expects professional, respectful participation. Technical criticism should address evidence, reasoning, interfaces, and outcomes rather than personal attributes or motives.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, and the publication of another person's private information are not permitted. Participants must protect credentials, personal data, vulnerability details, private research, and other sensitive information.

This code applies in repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other official Janus Governance spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove content, pause discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect participants, sensitive information, or the integrity of the project.

Enforcement decisions are made by the maintainers responsible for the affected space, subject to the final human authority defined in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
